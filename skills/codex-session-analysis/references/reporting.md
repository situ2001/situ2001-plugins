# Comparison reporting

Present the requested analyses with:

- Findings and the important comparability caveat.
- Selected samples, user-input differences, and measurement definitions.
- Per-run latency/token table and justified aggregates when measuring execution.
- Cost decomposition, official sources, date, and assumptions when cost is requested.
- Chronological search traces and their practical impact when comparing search behavior.
- Source-backed correctness, omissions, and uncertainty when evaluating answers.
- Original session links and repository evidence links.

Compute ratios from unrounded values and state the denominator for savings. Avoid claiming population-level model superiority from one or two runs.
